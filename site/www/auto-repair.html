Services

What we work on

Diagnostics

Check engine lights, warning messages, noises and drivability problems. We scan every module and test before we replace parts.

Brakes

Pads, rotors, calipers, fluid and ABS problems.

Suspension & steering

Clunks, pulling, wandering and worn parts: control arms, struts, bushings, tie rods.

Alignment & tires

Four-wheel alignment with before-and-after readings, plus tire installs, rotation and repair.

A/C & heating

Warm air, weak airflow and leaks. We find the cause before we recharge.

Maintenance

Oil service, fluids, filters, belts, batteries and inspections.

European & performance

Subaru, Mitsubishi, BMW, Porsche, Audi and modified cars are our favorite work.

Collision

Body and collision repair. Send photos of the damage and we'll tell you what it needs.
