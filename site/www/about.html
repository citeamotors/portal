About us

Built straight

Citea Motors is a Las Vegas shop that sells hand-picked used cars, repairs most makes, and services Teslas, all under one roof.

Our mission

We take pride in the way we do business. Buying a car should be exciting, not stressful. We work with you to find the car that fits your needs and budget, not our sales goals.

How we work

Every car we sell goes through our own shop first. Every repair starts with an inspection, photos of what we find, and an estimate you approve on your phone before we touch anything.

Our team

Our team members are technicians and car people who like solving hard problems, from a check engine light to a fully built WRX STI.

All cars sold are as is. We do not offer refunds, warranties or other guarantees on cars sold unless specifically stated in writing.
